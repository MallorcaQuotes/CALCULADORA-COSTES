Construction & Real Estate Glossary

A bilingual reference of technical terms used in building, architecture, and property acquisition in Mallorca and the Balearic Islands.

📋 Planning & Legal Documents

Budget for Material Execution

PEM

The net construction cost of a project, excluding VAT, professional fees, and municipal taxes. It is the baseline figure used by architects to calculate their fees and by the Ayuntamiento to determine the ICIO tax. All parametric estimates on MallorcaQuotes are based on the PEM.

Basic Project (Proyecto Básico)

The first formal architectural document submitted to the Ayuntamiento to obtain the building licence (Licencia de Obras). It defines the general layout, volumes, and land use compliance but does not yet contain full structural or technical engineering details.

Executive Project (Proyecto de Ejecución)

The full technical construction document prepared after the building licence is granted. It includes all structural, electrical, plumbing, thermal insulation, and finishing specifications required to begin physical construction works.

Building Licence (Licencia de Obras)

LO

The municipal building permit issued by the Ayuntamiento authorising the start of construction. Obtaining it typically takes 6–18 months in the Balearic Islands and requires prior approval of the Proyecto Básico.

Tax on Construction, Installations and Works

ICIO

A municipal tax levied on the PEM of any construction or renovation project. In most Balearic municipalities the ICIO rate is between 2.5% and 4% of the PEM. It is paid to the Ayuntamiento when the building licence is issued.

Certificate of Occupancy (Cédula de Habitabilidad)

CH

The official document issued by the Balearic Government confirming that a property meets all habitability standards (minimum surface areas, light, ventilation, and sanitation). It is mandatory to obtain a utility supply contract (water, electricity, gas) and to legally occupy a new home.

Land Use Plan (Plan de Ordenación Urbana)

LPO / PGOU

The master planning instrument of each municipality that classifies land as urban, urbanisable, or protected rural (rústico). It determines maximum buildable area, plot coverage, height limits, and setbacks. Every construction project in Mallorca must comply with the PGOU of the relevant municipality.

Property Register Extract (Nota Simple)

A non-certified summary extract from the Spanish Property Registry (Registro de la Propiedad) showing the legal owner, plot boundaries, any mortgages, and registered encumbrances. Essential due-diligence document before any purchase or construction project.

Property Transfer Tax (Impuesto de Transmisiones Patrimoniales)

ITP

The tax paid by the buyer on the acquisition of a second-hand (resale) property in Spain. In the Balearic Islands it is graduated from 8% to 13% depending on the purchase price. It does not apply to new-build properties, where IVA (VAT) applies instead.

🏗️ Construction & Structure

Load-Bearing Structure (Estructura)

The primary skeletal framework of a building that bears and transfers all vertical and horizontal loads to the foundations. In contemporary Mallorcan villas it typically consists of reinforced concrete pillars, beams, and flat slabs, often combined with traditional stone masonry load-bearing walls.

Foundations (Cimentación)

The underground structural elements that transfer building loads safely to the ground. Common types in the Balearics include spread footings (zapatas aisladas), strip footings (zapatas corridas), and pile systems for rocky or uneven terrain. Foundation type is defined in the geotechnical study.

Facade & Enclosure System (Cerramiento)

The external envelope system of a building, comprising exterior walls, cladding, thermal insulation, and window/door frames. In premium Mallorcan construction this often includes ventilated stone facades (Santanyí limestone), ETICS thermal systems, and high-performance aluminium joinery.

Interior Partition Walls (Partición Interior)

Non-structural walls that divide the internal floor plan. They can be built from lightweight plasterboard systems (Pladur) or traditional single-leaf brick. Acoustic performance and fire rating vary depending on material choice and room function.

Roof / Flat Roof (Cubierta)

The upper weatherproof envelope of a building. In modern Mallorcan villa design, flat roofs (cubiertas planas) are predominant, often designed as usable terraces with porcelain or natural stone paving over a waterproof membrane and thermal insulation layer.

Geotechnical Study (Estudio Geotécnico)

A subsurface soil and rock investigation report required before designing foundations. It determines bearing capacity, water table depth, rock type, and seismic risk. In the Balearic Islands, rocky calcareous terrain is common but can present irregular bearing conditions that significantly influence foundation costs.

Plot Development Works (Urbanización)

External infrastructure works required to connect a plot to municipal services: road access, water mains, sewer connections, and street lighting. In rural (rústico) plots in Mallorca these connections often involve substantial additional cost not included in the main construction PEM.

⚙️ Installations & Technical Systems

Air-Source Heat Pump (Aerotermia)

A high-efficiency HVAC technology that extracts thermal energy from outdoor air to provide heating, cooling, and domestic hot water. With a COP of 3–5, it is the preferred sustainable climate system in new Mallorcan villas and qualifies for EU energy efficiency subsidies. Our calculator includes aerotermia as the default premium HVAC option.

Underfloor Heating (Suelo Radiante)

A radiant heating system with a network of water pipes or electric cables embedded in the floor screed, distributing heat uniformly across the entire floor surface. It works at low water temperatures (28–35 °C), making it highly compatible with heat pumps. Widely specified in high-end Mallorcan villas for comfort and efficiency.

Photovoltaic Solar Panels (Instalación Fotovoltaica)

A renewable electricity generation system using silicon panels mounted on roofs or ground-mounted structures to convert solar radiation into direct current (DC), which is then inverted to AC for residential use. With over 300 days of sunshine per year, Mallorca offers excellent solar yield. Battery storage systems are increasingly paired with PV installations.

Smart Home / Home Automation (Domótica)

An integrated building control system managing lighting, climate, security, blinds, audio-visual equipment, and access control via a centralised interface (smartphone app, wall panel, or voice control). In premium Mallorcan villas, KNX and Control4 systems are commonly specified.

Drainage & Sewage System (Saneamiento)

The network of pipes, traps, and connections that collect and evacuate waste water and rainwater from a building to the municipal sewer network or, in rural plots, to a registered septic tank (fosa séptica) or treatment plant. Sizing is governed by the Spanish CTE-HS 5 technical building code.

Technical Building Inspection (Inspección Técnica de Edificios)

ITE

A periodic mandatory inspection of existing buildings over 45 years old, assessing the structural condition of facades, roofs, foundations, and common areas. Required in the Balearic Islands before selling or renovating older properties.

Energy Efficiency Certificate (Certificado de Eficiencia Energética)

CEE

A mandatory official document that rates the energy performance of a building from A (most efficient) to G (least efficient). It is required for all new constructions and for any property sale or rental in Spain. The Balearic Islands incentivise new builds achieving an A or B energy rating.

🏊 Finishes & Outdoor Spaces

Microcement (Microcemento)

A thin polymer-modified cementitious coating (2–3 mm) applied over existing substrates to achieve a seamless, continuous finish on floors, walls, and even wet areas. Signature material in contemporary Mallorcan interior design for its minimalist aesthetic. Requires professional application and periodic sealing.

Dry Stone Masonry (Pedra en Sec)

Traditional Mallorcan technique of constructing walls, terraces, and boundary enclosures using local limestone without any mortar. A UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage technique, it is widely used in high-end rural villa projects for boundary walls, retaining walls, and rustic facades. Premium execution can cost €250–€500 per linear metre.

Infinity Pool (Piscina Desbordante)

A swimming pool design where water overflows over one or more edges into a hidden collection channel, creating the visual illusion of merging with the horizon or landscape. Iconic in luxury Mallorcan villa design for sea-view plots. Requires a separate water collection tank and recirculation pump, adding cost compared to conventional pools. MallorcaQuotes pool pricing covers conventional in-ground pools; an infinity edge adds approximately 25–40% to the pool budget.

Large-Format Porcelain Flooring (Solado Porcelánico)

High-fired porcelain tile, often in formats of 60×60 cm to 120×120 cm or larger slabs, used for interior and exterior flooring. Preferred for its durability, low maintenance, and UV resistance in Mediterranean climates. Rectified large-format tiles require precision installation with minimal grout joints.

Exterior Joinery & Glazing (Carpintería Exterior)

Windows, sliding doors, and fixed glazing frames. Premium Mallorcan specifications favour thermally broken aluminium frames with double or triple-pane low-emissivity glass (Ug ≤ 1.1 W/m²K). Large sliding/folding systems (e.g., Schüco, Technal) connecting interior to terraces are a hallmark of luxury villa design and a significant cost line item (€800–€1,500 per m² installed).

Pergola & Shade Structures (Pérgola)

Freestanding or wall-attached outdoor structures providing shade and defining exterior living zones. Options range from timber pergolas with climbing plants to architectural bioclimatic pergolas with motorised adjustable louvres. In Mallorca's climate, exterior shade structures are key for extending outdoor usability through summer.

Landscaping & Garden Design (Paisajismo)

The design and implementation of exterior garden spaces, including grading and topsoil preparation, irrigation systems, planting design, and hard landscaping (paths, terraces, retaining walls). Mediterranean xeriscape design using drought-resistant native plants (olive trees, rosemary, lavender, bougainvillea) is increasingly preferred for sustainability and low water consumption.

Exterior Lighting (Alumbrado Exterior)

An architectural outdoor lighting scheme integrating path lights, pool illumination, uplighting for trees and facades, and accent lighting for terraces. In premium Mallorcan villas, DALI or DMX-controlled LED systems integrated with the smart home (domótica) are standard in high-quality specifications.

Premium Interior Finishes (Acabados Premium)

High-end interior finishing specification encompassing natural stone cladding (marble, travertine, limestone), bespoke joinery, solid oak or engineered wood flooring, designer sanitaryware, and custom kitchen cabinetry. Premium finishes can represent 20–35% of total project PEM and are the most significant variable between quality tiers in our calculator's estimates.

External Thermal Insulation Composite System

ETICS / SATE

An external wall insulation system bonding rigid insulation boards (EPS or mineral wool) to the structural wall, finished with a reinforced render coat. It eliminates thermal bridges and dramatically improves energy efficiency. Required in new constructions to meet the Spanish CTE-HE energy standard.

Waterproofing (Impermeabilización)

Membranes, coatings, or sheet systems applied to flat roofs, underground walls, pool shells, and wet rooms to prevent water infiltration. In Mallorca's climate, flat roofs and outdoor terraces require robust waterproofing systems, typically liquid polyurethane or TPO/EPDM membranes, with a guarantee of at least 10 years.
