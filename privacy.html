Privacy Policy (GDPR Compliance)

Information on the processing of personal data in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Data Controller

The data controller for the processing of personal data collected through this website is MallorcaQuotes (hereinafter, "the Controller"), operating from the Balearic Islands, Spain. For any inquiry regarding your personal information, you can contact our technical team via the contact form on this website.

2. Purpose of Processing and Legal Basis

MallorcaQuotes processes personal data provided by users (such as name, email address, phone number, and project parameters) strictly for the following purposes:

  • To calculate, process, and deliver customized indicative construction estimates and itemized architectural budgets requested by the user.
  • To respond to direct technical inquiries and project plan submissions.
  • To manage communications strictly related to the requested construction consultation.

The legal basis for processing this data is the explicit and unambiguous consent given by the user upon submitting the estimation or contact form (Art. 6.1.a GDPR).

3. Data Retention and Recipients

Personal data will only be kept for as long as necessary to fulfill the requested consultation or until the user exercises their right of erasure. Data is transmitted securely via Formspree Inc. (acting as a data processor adhering to standard contractual clauses) and is never sold, leased, or disclosed to third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

4. User Rights (ARCO-POL)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, users have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal data stored by MallorcaQuotes.
  • Request the rectification of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request the deletion (erasure) of their data when it is no longer needed.
  • Request the limitation of processing or object to data processing.
  • Request data portability.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority (in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos - AEPD).
